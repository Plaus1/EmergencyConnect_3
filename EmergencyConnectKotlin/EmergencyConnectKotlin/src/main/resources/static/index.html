BE PREPARED

Safety resources

General guidance only. Always follow instructions from official emergency responders.

01

Fire

Leave the building using a safe exit, stay low if there is smoke and never use lifts. Do not re-enter until authorities say it is safe.

02

Medical emergency

Contact emergency medical services. Give the exact location and follow the call handler’s instructions. Do not move a seriously injured person unless there is immediate danger.

03

Floods and storms

Move away from floodwater, avoid crossing flooded roads and seek higher ground when instructed. Keep away from fallen power lines.

04

Prepare a go-bag

Keep water, essential medication, a torch, a charged power bank, basic first-aid supplies, copies of key documents and emergency contacts ready.

Make a family emergency plan

Choose a meeting point, an out-of-area contact and a way to check in if mobile networks are disrupted.
